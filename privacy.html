Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information across quadcorp.co.za and our Ticked service.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy describes what QuadCorp actually collects and how it's actually used, in plain language. It's provided as a good-faith summary of our practices, not a substitute for formal legal advice — if you need a policy certified for a specific regulatory purpose, please have it reviewed by a qualified attorney.

1. Who we are

QuadCorp ("QuadCorp", "we", "us", "our") is a South African software studio operating at quadcorp.co.za. We build CRM software, websites and funnels, online stores, and Ticked, an automated WhatsApp booking assistant offered as a QuadCorp service. This policy covers all of these, including the Ticked waitlist and application pages hosted under quadcorp.co.za/Ticked.

2. Information we collect

Contacting us directly

Our contact page asks for your name, business name, email address, and project details. This form does not submit to a server — it prepares a WhatsApp message with what you've typed, which opens in WhatsApp for you to review and send. We only receive it once you choose to send that message, the same as any other WhatsApp conversation.

Joining the Ticked waitlist

The Ticked waitlist form collects your full name, business name, business type, and WhatsApp number. This is submitted directly to our systems and stored securely so we can reach out about early access.

Applying to onboard with Ticked

If you apply to use Ticked, we collect more detailed information needed to set up the service on your behalf: your business name, contact email and phone number, the WhatsApp number bookings should alert, the services you offer (names, duration, pricing), your business hours, your cancellation and rescheduling policies, any other operating notes you provide, and your chosen plan. We also collect the names, roles, email addresses and services of the team members you add, and the email address each person uses to sign in to the Ticked dashboard. We record what administrators and users do in the dashboard in an audit log.

Automatically collected information

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically logs standard technical information for security and reliability purposes — such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site, and we do not use cookies to track you across other websites.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes or consultations
  • To contact you about your Ticked waitlist spot or application
  • To configure and operate the Ticked service for your business, including connecting your WhatsApp number and Google Calendar so Ticked can manage bookings on your behalf
  • To send booking-related alerts (new bookings, changes, cancellations) to the WhatsApp number you provide
  • To maintain the security and reliability of our website and services

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We share it only where necessary to run the services you've asked for:

  • Vercel — our hosting provider, which stores waitlist submissions and serves our website and application infrastructure.
  • WhatsApp / Meta — messages you send us, and messages Ticked sends on a business's behalf, pass through WhatsApp's platform and are subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Calendar — if you use Ticked, your calendar is connected so Ticked can check availability and create bookings. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy in addition to this one.
  • Ticked's application-processing system — onboarding applications are submitted to the backend system that powers Ticked, so our team can review and configure your account.
  • Supabase — our database provider, where Ticked's data is stored (hosted in London, United Kingdom).
  • Anthropic — its AI service generates the replies Ticked sends to customers.
  • Google Fonts — our pages load fonts from Google's servers, which involves a request from your browser to Google as part of normal font delivery.

We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data storage and security

Information is stored with our hosting and database providers, Vercel and Supabase. Some of these providers process information outside South Africa, including in the United Kingdom and the United States. Waitlist and application data is kept in access-controlled storage; our admin views are protected by authentication, and we take reasonable technical measures to protect information from unauthorised access. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and application information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, provide the service you've requested, or meet legal and accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete your information at any time using the contact details below. For information about a Ticked business's customers, see the section on Ticked and information about our clients' customers.

7. Your rights

As a South African business, we handle personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Depending on where you're based, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of your information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. We'll respond within a reasonable time. You can also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za. To have your information deleted, see How to delete your data.

8. Cookies

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. Some pages use your browser's local storage purely for on-page functionality (for example, remembering a UI preference); this data stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for business owners and operators. We don't knowingly collect information from children, and our services are not directed at them.

10. Third-party links

Our site links to third-party platforms including Google and Facebook (for reviews) and WhatsApp. Once you leave our site, that platform's own privacy policy applies — we're not responsible for their practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of our site or services after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Ticked and information about our clients' customers

Who is responsible for what

When a business uses Ticked, that business decides why and how its customers' information is used. It is the "responsible party" under POPIA. QuadCorp acts as its "operator": we process the information only on the business's behalf, to provide Ticked, and under its instructions. For information about the businesses themselves (their applications, logins and team members), QuadCorp is the responsible party, as described in the rest of this policy.

What we process

When a customer of a Ticked business messages that business's WhatsApp number, Ticked processes:

  • their WhatsApp number and the name on their WhatsApp profile;
  • the text of their messages and the replies sent (images and voice notes are not processed);
  • booking details: the service, date and time, the team member, and whether the booking was confirmed, moved or cancelled;
  • the matching entry on the business's calendar, which includes their name, the service and the time; and
  • when a reminder or follow-up was sent, and whether they tapped Confirm or Cancel.

Why we process it

Only to run the business's booking service: to answer questions about the business, make, move and cancel bookings, send reminders and follow-ups, tell the business owner and team members about their bookings, and give the business its own booking statistics. We do not use customers' messages for marketing, we do not sell them, and we do not use them to train models.

Automated replies

Ticked replies to customers using an AI assistant. It reads the recent messages in the conversation, replies, and can make, move or cancel appointments. The business can read every conversation, and on its Pro plan can take over a conversation at any time.

Who we share it with

Only the providers needed to run the service:

  • Meta (WhatsApp): delivers the messages.
  • Google: where the business connects its calendar.
  • Supabase: our database provider, hosted in London, United Kingdom.
  • Vercel: hosts our applications.
  • Anthropic: its AI service generates the assistant's replies.

We share it with no one else, except where the law requires it.

Where it is processed

Some of these providers process information outside South Africa, including in the United Kingdom and the United States. We rely on each provider's contractual and legal commitments to protect personal information.

Security

  • Each business can only see its own customers' information. Team members only see their own bookings and clients.
  • Connections to Ticked are encrypted in transit.
  • Access to our systems is restricted, and what our administrators do is recorded in an audit log.
  • If we become aware of a security breach affecting a business's customers' information, we tell that business without delay, so it can meet its own duties to notify the Information Regulator and the people affected.

How long we keep it

We keep a business's customer information for as long as that business uses Ticked, so the assistant has context and the business has its records. When a business stops using Ticked, we delete or return the information as agreed with that business. After a business cancels, it has 3 months to rejoin. After that, we permanently delete its information, including its customers' information, from our live systems, and the business must re-apply to use Ticked again. We keep only the records we are legally required to keep, such as invoices and payment records. In-app notifications are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Your rights as a customer of a Ticked business

The business is your first point of contact for requests about your information. You may also contact us at quadcorp.co.za@gmail.com. You can ask what information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. We can export or delete a customer's records for the business concerned on request.

How to delete your data

To have your information deleted, email quadcorp.co.za@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Tell us the WhatsApp number or email address your information is held under and, if you are a customer, the name of the business you messaged. We confirm who you are, then either delete what we hold or pass the request to the business that is responsible for it. We reply within 30 days. A business can also ask us to delete everything held for its account. If the law requires us to keep something, we will tell you.

13. How Ticked uses Google user data

What we access

When a business connects its Google Calendar to Ticked, it gives us permission to use Google Calendar on its behalf. Ticked uses this to:

  • check when the business and its team members are free;
  • create, change and delete the appointment entries Ticked makes; and
  • create a separate calendar for each team member inside the business's own Google account, which can be shared with that team member.

What we store

We store a secure connection token, so Ticked keeps working without the owner signing in each time. We also store the identifiers of the calendars and entries Ticked creates, so it can change or cancel them. We do not keep a copy of the other entries in the owner's calendar.

How we use it

Only to provide the booking features the business has asked for. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to develop or train AI models.

Who can see it

We do not transfer Google user data to anyone else, except to provide the booking service the business asked for (including the providers described above), to keep it secure or comply with the law, or with the business's consent. Our team only looks at a business's bookings with its permission, to provide support, to keep the service secure, or to meet a legal duty.

Google's requirements

Ticked's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting

A business can disconnect Ticked at any time in its Google Account, under third-party apps and services. Ticked can then no longer read or change the calendar. Entries already made stay in the calendar until the owner deletes them. To have the stored connection token and the identifiers we hold deleted as well, email quadcorp.co.za@gmail.com.
