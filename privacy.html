Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information across quadcorp.co.za and our Ticked service.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy describes what QuadCorp actually collects and how it's actually used, in plain language. It's provided as a good-faith summary of our practices, not a substitute for formal legal advice — if you need a policy certified for a specific regulatory purpose, please have it reviewed by a qualified attorney.

1. Who we are

QuadCorp ("QuadCorp", "we", "us", "our") is a South African software studio operating at quadcorp.co.za. We build CRM software, websites and funnels, online stores, and Ticked, an automated WhatsApp booking assistant offered as a QuadCorp service. This policy covers all of these, including the Ticked waitlist and application pages hosted under quadcorp.co.za/Ticked.

2. Information we collect

Contacting us directly

Our contact page asks for your name, business name, email address, and project details. This form does not submit to a server — it prepares a WhatsApp message with what you've typed, which opens in WhatsApp for you to review and send. We only receive it once you choose to send that message, the same as any other WhatsApp conversation.

Joining the Ticked waitlist

The Ticked waitlist form collects your full name, business name, business type, and WhatsApp number. This is submitted directly to our systems and stored securely so we can reach out about early access.

Applying to onboard with Ticked

If you apply to use Ticked, we collect more detailed information needed to set up the service on your behalf: your business name, contact email and phone number, the WhatsApp number bookings should alert, the services you offer (names, duration, pricing), your business hours, your cancellation and rescheduling policies, any other operating notes you provide, and your chosen plan.

Automatically collected information

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically logs standard technical information for security and reliability purposes — such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site, and we do not use cookies to track you across other websites.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes or consultations
  • To contact you about your Ticked waitlist spot or application
  • To configure and operate the Ticked service for your business, including connecting your WhatsApp number and Google Calendar so Ticked can manage bookings on your behalf
  • To send booking-related alerts (new bookings, changes, cancellations) to the WhatsApp number you provide
  • To maintain the security and reliability of our website and services

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We share it only where necessary to run the services you've asked for:

  • Vercel — our hosting provider, which stores waitlist submissions and serves our website and application infrastructure.
  • WhatsApp / Meta — messages you send us, and messages Ticked sends on your behalf once onboarded, pass through WhatsApp's own platform and are subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Calendar — if you use Ticked, your calendar is connected so Ticked can check availability and create bookings. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy in addition to this one.
  • Ticked's application-processing system — onboarding applications are submitted to the backend system that powers Ticked, so our team can review and configure your account.
  • Google Fonts — our pages load fonts from Google's servers, which involves a request from your browser to Google as part of normal font delivery.

We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data storage and security

Information is stored on Vercel's infrastructure. Waitlist and application data is kept in access-controlled storage; our admin views are protected by authentication, and we take reasonable technical measures to protect information from unauthorised access. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and application information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, provide the service you've requested, or meet legal and accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete your information at any time using the contact details below.

7. Your rights

As a South African business, we handle personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Depending on where you're based, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of your information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

8. Cookies

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. Some pages use your browser's local storage purely for on-page functionality (for example, remembering a UI preference); this data stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for business owners and operators. We don't knowingly collect information from children, and our services are not directed at them.

10. Third-party links

Our site links to third-party platforms including Google and Facebook (for reviews) and WhatsApp. Once you leave our site, that platform's own privacy policy applies — we're not responsible for their practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of our site or services after a change means you accept the updated policy.
