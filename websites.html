FAQ

Websites, answered.

How long does a website take to build?

A one-pager can ship in days. Larger multi-page builds typically take two to four weeks depending on scope and revisions.

Is copywriting included?

We'll refine and structure the copy you provide, and can write it from scratch for an additional fee if you'd rather we handled it end to end.

How many revisions do I get?

Revision rounds are included depending on your package, listed on each plan above. Extra rounds can always be added if needed.

Do you handle hosting and the domain too?

Yes. Domain, SSL, and hosting are all set up for you, with optional ongoing hosting plans so you never have to think about it.
