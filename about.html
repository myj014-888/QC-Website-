FAQ

About QuadCorp.

Do you only work with businesses in one region?

No. QuadCorp works with businesses wherever they're growing. Our systems are cloud-native and our process runs entirely on calls, shared docs, and messaging, so where you're based has never limited who we work with.

What industries do you work with?

Mostly service businesses, e-commerce brands, and growing teams that need CRM, web, or store infrastructure that actually holds up. If you're not sure we're a fit, the fastest way to find out is a quick consultation call.

Can you take on ongoing work after the first build?

Yes, most of our client relationships continue well past launch. We offer ongoing support and iteration plans so your systems keep evolving as your business does.

How do payments and contracts work?

Every project starts with a clear, written scope and quote before any work begins, so there are no surprises on either side. Payment terms are agreed upfront on a per-project basis.
