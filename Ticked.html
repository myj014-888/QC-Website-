Questions

The things
everyone asks.

What is Ticked Essential?

It is the plan Ticked launches with: the assistant answering and booking on your WhatsApp, reminders and follow-ups, and your dashboard with every chat, booking and client. We set it up and test it with you. Joining the waitlist is free and commits you to nothing; we talk you through the details when we come back to you.

Do my clients need to download anything?

No. They message your business on WhatsApp exactly as they do now. There is no app, no link and no form.

Does it use my existing WhatsApp number?

Ticked runs on a dedicated business number that is not already signed in to the WhatsApp app — usually a spare SIM. We set it up with you during onboarding, and your clients message that number.

What if it gets something wrong?

You see every conversation in your dashboard, and you can step into any chat and type yourself; the assistant goes quiet until you hand it back. It also never invents a time — it books only against your real calendar and the hours you set.

Which calendar does it work with?

Google Calendar. Each team member gets their own Ticked calendar inside your account, and anything you block there is treated as busy.

What happens to my clients' information?

It stays yours. We hold it to run the service for you and for nothing else, and it is never used to train an AI model. You can export or delete a customer's record at any time. The detail is in our Privacy Policy.

How long until it is working?

Once you are off the waitlist, we build your assistant and send your logins. The parts that take time are approving your business number with WhatsApp and connecting your calendar — we walk you through both and test a real booking with you before you go live.

Can I cancel?

Yes, it is month to month. If you leave, your data is kept for three months in case you come back, and then permanently deleted apart from invoices we are legally required to keep.
