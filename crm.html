FAQ

CRM, answered.

How long does setup actually take?

Most CRM builds are live within one to two weeks, depending on how many automations and integrations you need from day one.

Can you migrate my existing client data?

Yes. Spreadsheets, another CRM, or a messy mix of both, we'll map and migrate your existing data so nothing gets lost in the switch.

Is it a monthly contract or can I cancel anytime?

Plans are billed monthly with no long-term lock-in. If it's not working for you, you can walk away without a penalty.

Do I need technical skills to manage it day-to-day?

No. It's built for your whole team to use from day one, and we include training when we hand it over.
