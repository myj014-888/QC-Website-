FAQ

Online stores, answered.

Can you migrate my existing store?

Yes, products, customers, and order history can all be migrated across so you don't start from zero.

How long does a store take to build?

A standard Shopify build typically takes two to four weeks. Fully custom builds are scoped individually based on complexity.

What ongoing costs should I expect?

Beyond your platform and hosting fees, ongoing costs depend on whether you want us managing updates, or just on call when you need us.

Will I be able to update products myself?

Yes. Every store ships with a simple dashboard for managing your catalogue, no developer required for day-to-day changes.
